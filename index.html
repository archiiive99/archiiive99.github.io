I am a Ph.D. student working with Prof. Soonmin Hwang on representation learning for autonomous driving. I started my doctoral studies in September 2026, after receiving my B.S. in Computer Engineering from Dongguk University and my M.S. in Automotive Engineering from Hanyang University. Previously, I interned with the VVSW & Perception Unit at RideFlux, working on ML-based planning, scenario tagging, and MLOps. During my undergraduate studies, I conducted medical imaging research. My research interests include End-to-End Autonomous Driving, Closed-loop Evaluation, 3D Gaussian Splatting, and Planning-aligned Pretraining.
